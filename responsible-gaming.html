Responsible Gaming

DiwaSlot is designed for entertainment purposes. We are committed to promoting responsible play among our users.

Age Restriction

DiwaSlot is intended strictly for users aged 18 and above.

Tips for Responsible Play

  • Set a time and budget limit before you start playing
  • Never play with money you cannot afford to lose
  • Take regular breaks
  • Do not use gaming as a way to solve financial problems
  • If gaming stops being fun, take a break

Getting Help

If you feel that gaming is becoming a problem for you or someone you know, please consider reaching out to a local support organization for responsible gambling assistance in your region.
