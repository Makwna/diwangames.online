How to Play

Getting started with DiwaSlot is quick and simple. Follow the steps below to download and start playing.

  1. 1. Download the App

    Visit our homepage and click the download button to get the DiwaSlot app on your device.

  2. 2. Create an Account

    Open the app and sign up using your mobile number or email address. Make sure you meet the minimum age requirement of 18 years.

  3. 3. Explore the Games

    Browse through our collection of games and choose the one you'd like to play.

  4. 4. Play Responsibly

    Set a budget for yourself and always play for entertainment purposes only. Read our Responsible Gaming page for tips.
